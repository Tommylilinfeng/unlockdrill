How we know: 12 interviews, 5 Reddit threads (round 2 in progress), and the thing every blocker user told us: the lock gets ignored.

The moment that changed us: I04. We dropped the lock and kept the question.

Name the audience the same way every teammate does. Name the moment a user changed the direction.

Solution

Meet them at the open. One question. Skip is always there.

Position: not a blocker. An opt-in offer at the moment the thumb already goes.

How: the iPhone’s own Shortcuts automation: when Instagram opens, open UnlockDrill. No app store, no Screen Time permission.

Then: one question, one-line why, back to Instagram. Pause and frequency are the user’s.

Cut: accounts, streaks, feeds, score promises.

Faked for now: welcome email by hand; question review by hand.

Real: everything you are about to see.

Reason from the root problem (starting) to the position (offer, not lock).
